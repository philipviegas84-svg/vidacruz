Daily Brief Lite

Every morning at 8:00, Slack shows your portfolio value, today's move, your best and worst holding, your progress to your FIRE number and the headlines that matter. You build it once, in about 30 minutes, on free plans.

Time to set up≈ 30 min
CostAED 0
Make credits≈ 6 a day
CodingNone
⏰ 8:00→Google Sheets→Text aggregator→RSS news→Text aggregator→Gemini→Slack

The sheet does all the maths with Google's free price formulas. Gemini only writes the words, using the numbers it is given.

What you need

Google account FreeFor Google Sheets. Any Gmail account works.
Slack FreeA workspace you own. Create one at slack.com/get-started if you don't have one.
Make.com FreeThe free plan gives 1,000 credits a month and 2 active scenarios. This brief uses about 180 a month.
Gemini API key FreeFrom Google AI Studio. The free tier is enough. Google may use free-tier prompts to improve its products; this brief only sends prices and headlines. If you'd rather it didn't, turn on billing for the key (it costs cents a month).

Part 1 · Your portfolio sheet

  1. Open the template: Make a copy of the Daily Brief Lite sheet. Google asks to make a copy; click Make a copy. It lands in your Drive as Copy of Daily Brief Lite – My Portfolio (template). Rename it My Portfolio.
  2. Replace the five example rows (rows 2–6) with your holdings. Fill only the four white columns: Ticker, Name, Quantity, Avg cost. Everything else calculates by itself. There is room for 10 holdings; to add more, insert rows above row 11 and copy row 10's formulas down.
  3. In J14, type your FIRE number from the VidaCruz calculator (for example 4500000).
  4. Check column K (Brief line): every holding should read like NASDAQ:AAPL (Apple) | price 347.73 USD | today -0.1% | …. If a line says no price found, fix the ticker.
You holdType the ticker like this
US stocksNASDAQ:AAPL, NYSE:JPM
US ETFsNYSEARCA:VOO, NASDAQ:QQQ
IndiaNSE:INFY, NSE:RELIANCE
LondonLON:HSBA (prices in pence)
CryptoCURRENCY:BTCUSD, CURRENCY:ETHUSD (no daily % change)
UAE (DFM / ADX)Google's formula doesn't cover most UAE shares. Type the price into the Price cell yourself and set Currency to AED. The paid Daily Brief Pro reads UAE prices for you.
Not sure of a ticker? Search the company on google.com/finance: the code shown under its name (like NASDAQ: NVDA) is what goes in the sheet, without the space.

Part 2 · Slack channel

  1. In your Slack workspace, click + next to Channels › Create a channel.
  2. Name it daily-brief, keep it Public (it's still only visible inside your workspace) and create it.

Part 3 · Gemini API key

  1. Go to aistudio.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Get API key › Create API key. Pick or create a project if asked.
  3. Copy the key (it starts with AIza). Keep it private: don't paste it into chats or emails.

Part 4 · Make.com account

  1. Sign up at make.com (free plan, no card needed).
  2. Set your time zone, or the brief arrives at the wrong hour: click your avatar (bottom left) › Profile › Time zone › Asia/Dubai (or yours) › Save changes.
  3. Go to Scenarios › Create a new scenario.
Shortcut: instead of adding each node by hand, download daily-brief-lite.blueprint.json (right-click › Save link as, if it opens as text), then in the empty scenario click ⋯ (bottom toolbar) › Import Blueprint › choose the file › Save. All six nodes appear with their settings. You still need to do the parts marked You choose below: your connections, your sheet and your channel.

Part 5 · The scenario, node by node

Add each node with the big + (or the small + to the right of the last node), search for the app, pick the action, then fill the fields exactly as shown. Text in {{ }} is a value from an earlier node: click into the field and pick it from the panel that opens (the number is the node's number in your scenario).

  1. Click Save (the disk icon), then Run once. Each node shows a bubble with a number when it worked.
  2. Check Slack: the brief should be in #daily-brief within a few seconds.
  3. Click the clock on the first node › Run scenario: Every day › Time: 08:00 › OK.
  4. Switch the scenario ON with the toggle at the bottom, and Save again.
That's it. From tomorrow, your brief arrives at 8:00 every day. It uses about 6 credits a run, so roughly 180 of your 1,000 free credits a month.

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
Node 1 shows an error about the sheetPick the spreadsheet again from the list, and check the Sheet Name matches the tab at the bottom of your sheet.
The brief says a price is missingColumn K shows no price found for that ticker: fix the ticker format (see Part 1).
Node 5 says the API key isn't validOpen node 5 › Connection › Edit, paste the key again with no spaces. Create a new key in AI Studio if needed.
Node 5 says quota exceededThe free Gemini tier has a daily limit. Wait until tomorrow, or turn on billing for the key in AI Studio.
Node 6 can't find the channelMake sure the channel is public, or invite the Make app to it by typing /invite @Make in the channel.
It arrives at the wrong timeSet your time zone in your Make profile (Part 4, step 2).
No headlinesOpen the RSS URL in your browser: if it shows nothing, change the search words.
