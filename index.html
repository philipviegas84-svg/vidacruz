Questions

Before you start

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You import a ready blueprint into Make.com and fill in the fields the guide shows you. The code inside the paid kits is already written; you only change a few marked lines such as your tickers or job titles.

What will it cost me each month?

The free Daily Brief Lite runs at AED 0 on free plans. Paid agents need Make's Core plan (about US$9 a month) plus a few cents to a few dollars of AI use, depending on which agents you pick. The order slip above estimates it for your selection.

Is this financial advice?

No. The agents summarise your own numbers and the news, for your information. They don't tell you what to buy or sell.

Where does my data go?

Into your own accounts: your Google Sheet, your Make scenarios, your Slack. VidaCruz never sees your portfolio, emails or keys.

What if a tool changes?

Tools change their models and limits from time to time. Kit buyers get updates for 12 months, and the Monthly partner plan covers fixes for you.

Can I use WhatsApp or Telegram instead of Slack?

Today the kits post to Slack. Tell us in the form if you'd prefer WhatsApp, Telegram or email: if enough people ask, we'll add it.
